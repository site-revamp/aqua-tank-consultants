# Aqua Tank Consultants Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Aqua Tank Consultants provides water tank cleaning, pump maintenance, inspections, repairs, and water-system components for Singapore buildings. The proposed homepage helps MCSTs, facilities teams, and property operators identify the relevant service and request a quotation.

## Main Improvements

1. **Use one confirmed contact number throughout.** The homepage and older Contact page display different numbers. The redesign would use the confirmed primary number consistently in the header, footer, quotation forms, and mobile Call and WhatsApp buttons, helping customers reach the same team from every enquiry point.
2. **Explain the specialist capabilities more clearly.** Replace broad company statements with supported details about licensed-plumber supervision, PUB-related documentation and certification support, confined-space-trained personnel, and maintenance records. This would give building managers more relevant information when assessing the service.
3. **Organise services consistently.** Align the homepage and service navigation around tank cleaning and sterilisation, pump maintenance, pump repair and replacement, and tank inspection and repair. Include pantry pump servicing and link each major service to its detail page and a relevant enquiry action.

## Design Improvements

Clear service headings and consistent summaries would make the offering easier to scan. A dedicated capabilities section would keep supervision and documentation details together, while prominent contact actions would give visitors a direct next step.
