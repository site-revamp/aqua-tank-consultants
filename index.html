PUB compliance

Keep your water tank inspection and certification on schedule.

For applicable water service installations with storage tanks, PUB requirements call for inspection at least once every 12 months by a Licensed Plumber, with cleaning and disinfection where necessary, followed by certification.

Aqua Tank's published service describes licensed-plumber supervision, PUB notification and official certification for building records.

Frequently asked questions

Helpful answers for building managers.

How often must water tanks be inspected in Singapore?

PUB states that the responsible person for a water service installation with a storage tank must engage a Licensed Plumber at least once every 12 months to inspect and, where necessary, clean and disinfect the tank and certify it.

Will the water supply be shut off during tank cleaning?

Not necessarily. Aqua Tank explains that tanks with two compartments can often be cleaned one cell at a time. For single-compartment tanks, the work can be planned with building management to minimise interruption.

Do I receive certification after tank cleaning?

Aqua Tank states that its licensed plumbers inspect the installation, oversee sterilisation, handle PUB notification and issue official certification for building records.

Which pump brands does Aqua Tank service?

The company lists experience with Grundfos, Wilo, Ebara and Lowara pump systems.

Does Aqua Tank provide emergency support?

Which areas does Aqua Tank serve?
